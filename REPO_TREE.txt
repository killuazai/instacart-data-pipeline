Notes:
  - src/ contains reviewable or reusable versions of individual pipeline tasks.
  - tests/ contains the extracted layer-validation and reconciliation tasks.
  - dashboards/Instacart Business Insights.lvdash.json is aligned with the current
    workspace.instacart_analytics tables and is the canonical dashboard export.
  - The root-level Instacart Customer & Purchase Analytics.lvdash.json and
    src/Instacart Star Schema with Constraints.ipynb reference an earlier or
    alternative model and are not part of the four-notebook execution path.
